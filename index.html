🌾 Farmer Theory

When does pausing capital settler-spam to build a Farmer specialist on a grain farm pay off — in settlers out. Now configurable by starting family, leader archetype, and leader strength. Every number traced to Old World's XML & C# source (…/Old World/Reference/).

TL;DR
  • It's a build-queue question. One queue (City.cs mllBuildQueue); only the front item builds, queued items decay. Settler (from GROWTH) and Farmer (from CIVICS) are mutually exclusive — the Farmer pauses settlers for its build time = ceil(farmer civics cost ÷ civics rate). Banking civics does not speed it.
  • The citizen is free. Cities start with 1 citizen that otherwise produces nothing, so the first Farmer eats it for free.
  • Landowners change everything. +2.0 growth city-wide and −50% rural-specialist train time → the Farmer costs 20 civics, not 40 (≈2 turns, not 4). That's why "with Landowners + granary + farmer" the second settler barely slips.
  • Leader strength barely matters here. No rating modifies GROWTH (ratings only %-boost civics/training/science/money). Strength only helps via Charisma → Civics → faster Farmer, and only for Charisma archetypes (Orator/Diplomat/Judge). Builder helps differently: −1 build turn on the worker (farm/granary online a turn sooner).

1 · Where every growth point comes from

Base +8, Connected +2, Governor +2 (any leader, iLeaderGovernor); grain farm +2.0 which the adjacent granary scales ×1.6 → +3.2; Granary flat +2.0; Farmer +1.0 plus 50%×3.2 = +1.6. Without Farmer = 17.2; with = 19.8 (Δ +2.6, plus a quiet +1.0 science). A growth-family like Landowners adds +2.0 on top of all of it.

Pre-granary the grain farm is only +2.0 (no ×1.6), so the city sits at 14.0 — your "GROWTH +14" screenshot.

4 · How to read it

  • Granary always. Worker-built, off-queue; it turns grain growth into 3.2 and is what makes the Farmer's half worth 1.6.
  • Landowners → Farmer is almost free. Half-cost Farmer (~2 queue-turns) + the +2 growth means the Farmer path barely slips the next settler and pulls ahead within a couple settlers. On a Landowners grain capital, just do it.
  • Non-growth family → it's the queue-time trade. Full 40-civics Farmer (~3–4 turns) vs +2.6 growth; break-even is a handful of settlers — still usually worth it on a settler capital.
  • Builder leader shaves a turn off farm & granary (worker), ramping growth earlier — small, free, helps both lines.
  • Strength is a near-no-op for settlers. Push it to 6 on a non-Charisma archetype and nothing changes — because no rating touches growth. It only matters as Charisma→Civics→Farmer-speed.
  • Don't stack Farmers early. Each specialist already built adds +5% to the next one's cost, and #2+ need a grown citizen (100+10·size growth) + add discontent. That's the "second, not always."

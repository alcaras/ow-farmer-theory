🌾 Farmer Theory

When does pausing capital settler-spam to build a Farmer specialist on a grain farm pay off — in settlers out. Configurable by starting family, leader archetype, leader traits, and empire growth modifiers. Every number traced to Old World's XML & C# source.

TL;DR
  • It's a build-queue question. One queue (City.cs mllBuildQueue); only the front item builds, queued items decay. Settler (from GROWTH) and Farmer (from CIVICS) are mutually exclusive — the Farmer pauses settlers for its build time = ceil(farmer civics cost ÷ civics rate). Banking civics does not speed it.
  • The citizen is free. Cities start with 1 citizen that otherwise produces nothing, so the first Farmer eats it for free.
  • Landowners change everything. +2.0 growth city-wide and −50% rural-specialist train time → the Farmer costs 20 civics, not 40 (≈2 turns, not 4). The second settler barely slips.
  • Leader effects are specific and grounded. No rating modifies GROWTH. Direct trait governor effects: Affable +growth, Eloquent +civics, Cultivator +50% farm — personality traits grant no rating (the aiRatingFallback on them is a stand-in for AI math, not a Charisma bonus). Charisma comes only from a Charismatic archetype (Orator/Diplomat/Judge), which %-boosts civics → faster Farmer. Builder archetype shaves a turn off the worker (farm/granary sooner), not the Farmer.

1 · Where every growth point comes from

Base +8, Connected +2, Governor +2 (any leader); grain farm +2.0 which the adjacent granary scales ×1.6 → +3.2; Granary flat +2.0; Farmer +1.0 plus 50%×3.2 = +1.6. Without Farmer = 17.2; with = 19.8 (Δ +2.6). Then Landowners +2, Affable +2, Cultivator (grain ×1.5), and +20% empire modifiers pile on.

Pre-granary the grain farm is only +2.0 (no ×1.6), so a Landowners-free city sits at 14.0 — your "GROWTH +14" screenshot.

4 · How to read it

  • Granary always. Worker-built, off-queue; it turns grain growth into 3.2 and makes the Farmer's half worth 1.6.
  • Landowners → Farmer is almost free. Half-cost Farmer (~2 queue-turns) + the +2 growth: the Farmer line barely slips the next settler and pulls ahead within a couple settlers.
  • Stack the growth. Cultivator (+50% farm), Affable (+2), and the +20% empire modifiers all multiply settler speed and make the Farmer pay back faster (its queue cost is fixed in civics).
  • Civics is the Farmer's clock. Eloquent (+2 civics), Patrons/Sages (+2 civics), and a Charismatic archetype's civics% (Orator/Diplomat/Judge) all shorten the queue pause. Builder does not — it only speeds the worker.
  • Don't stack Farmers early. Each specialist already built adds +5% to the next, and #2+ need a grown citizen (100+10·size growth) + add discontent.
